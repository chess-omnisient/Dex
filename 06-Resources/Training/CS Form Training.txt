CS Intake Form – Training Walkthrough

Hi all, I just wanted to give you a brief rundown of how the customer success intake form works. I've shared this link in the channel – so let's walk through the form.

The goal of this form is to give the customer success team as much detail as possible so they can move as quickly as possible.

**Request name**
Give the request a name. Example: "ABSA Overlap Report Test."

**Department**
Say which department the request is from: sales or data product.

**Sales owner**
This would be either [[Chris_Brown|Chris]] or [[Anton_Grutzmacher|Anton]].

**Requester**
The person making the request – put yourself for now.

**Client**
Who the request is for. In this example, it's ABSA.

**Region**
Select the region: South Africa, US, MENA, UK, Brazil, or APAC. We'll select South Africa.

**Data seller**
Choose from the dropdown of available data sellers. In this case, ShopRite.

**Datasets (optional)**
Not required, but adding relevant datasets helps the team going forward – e.g., "ABSA Dataset 012, ShopRite Dataset 86."

**Request type**
An info block shows the available types: managed services, platform support, sales and growth support, or data product growth. We'll select sales and growth support.

**Type of work**
Overlap, POC, contracted, or ad hoc request. In this case, it's an overlap.

**Task outcome**
Describe the desired result and any potential risks. Example: "ABSA requested an overlap. No risks."

**Task description**
A short summary of what needs to be delivered. Example: "Need an overlap report, Power BI, on the ABSA profile."

**Revenue (optional)**
Attach revenue linked to the opportunity if applicable – e.g., 1 million rand – plus an optional field for when that revenue is expected to land. This helps the team assess priority. Example: revenue expected next Friday, client wants delivery by 25 September.

**Urgency**
This week, next week, next sprint, or not urgent. Since the due date is 25 September, we'll select next sprint.

**Priority**
Urgent, high, normal, or low. We'll select normal for this one.

**Frequency**
Once-off or recurring task. In this case, once-off.

**Internal stakeholders**
Anyone on the CS team you depend on to complete the work. Example: VR needs to supply information to help with this process.

**Attachments**
Upload any relevant files, then click submit.

Once submitted, the request pings the CS team and they action it onto the appropriate sprint board.

Thank you.
