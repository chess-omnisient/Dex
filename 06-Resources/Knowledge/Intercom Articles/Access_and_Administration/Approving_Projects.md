# Step-by-Step Guide: Approving Projects Created on Your Data

Approve or reject projects created using your data.

Approving projects created on your data gives you control, visibility,
and peace of mind around how your data is being used. The process is
designed to be simple and transparent so you always stay informed.

When one of your data partners creates a project using your dataset,
you'll be notified either in-app or via email. Email notifications
include a direct link to the request. In this guide, we'll start from
the platform landing page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 1: Navigate to the Admin Section

-   Click on the **Admin** link in your side navigation.

⚠️ **Note:** If you don't see the Admin option, you do not have admin
permissions.\
Please contact one of your company admins to request access so you can
approve projects going forward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 2: Open the Approvals Page

-   Click on the **Approvals** sub-navigation link.

This opens the **Approvals page**, your dedicated hub for managing
approval requests.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 3: Review the Approvals Page

On the Approvals page, you'll see the **Project Approval table**, which
acts as your to-do list.

-   Each pending request appears in the table.
-   Once you approve or reject a request, it disappears from the list.
-   Only items requiring your attention remain visible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 4: Choose a Request to Review

In the table:

-   Look for the **Review** action in the last column.
-   The Review option appears for requests with an **"Awaiting
    Approval"** status.

If you see a **"Locked"** status: - The dataset share used by your
partner has expired. - This can happen if a request sits unreviewed for
too long.

💡 **Tip:** Action new requests promptly so your data partners receive
timely feedback and to prevent dataset shares from expiring.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 5: Review the Request Details

Clicking **Review** opens a modal that includes:

-   Information about the project request
-   Expanded details about the dataset you shared that was used to
    create the project

Take a moment to confirm everything looks correct before making your
decision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 6: Approve or Reject the Request

When ready:

### Approve

-   Click **Approve** to green-light the project.
-   The modal will close.
-   The requestor automatically receives an email confirming approval.
-   The project moves into processing.

### Reject

-   If something doesn't look right, select **Reject** instead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 7: Provide a Rejection Reason (If Applicable)

If you choose to reject the request:

-   A rejection modal will appear.
-   Enter a clear reason for rejecting the request.

This feedback: - Helps the requestor understand what went wrong -
Prevents repeated mistakes - Appears in the rejection email sent to them

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ✅ You're Done!

You've successfully managed a project approval.

Each approval ensures your data is used appropriately --- keeping you in
control while enabling secure collaboration with your partners.
