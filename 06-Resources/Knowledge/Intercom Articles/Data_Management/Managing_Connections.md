# How to request and manage connections

This guide covers how to request a new connection with a partner company and how to manage your existing connections on the Omnisient platform.

---

## Before you start

- **Permissions required:** Editor role with write access on your organisation's Omnisient account
- **Prerequisites:** The company you wish to connect with must be registered on the Omnisient platform

---

## Getting there

Log in to the Omnisient platform → **Data Collaboration** (side navigation) → **Connections**

You will see the **All Connections** screen showing a table of your existing connections with their partner name, creation date, and status.

---

## Requesting a new connection

1. On the All Connections screen, click **+ Request Connection** in the top right.
2. Enter the name of the company you wish to connect with. Ensure the name is spelled correctly to avoid delays.
3. Click **Send Request**. This notifies the Omnisient client support team, who will verify the company and forward your request to the partner.
4. Your connection will appear in the table with a **Requested** status while the partner reviews it.
5. Once the partner accepts, your company's admin must also approve the connection. Look for the connection with **Pending** status and click **Accept** to finalise.
6. When both parties have accepted, the status changes to **Ready** and a private collaboration space is established for securely sharing datasets.

> Only users with the appropriate permissions can approve incoming connection requests. Ensure the right people in your organisation are aware of pending requests.

---

## Understanding connection statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Requested** | You have submitted a connection request. It is being processed by the Omnisient team before reaching the partner. |
| **Awaiting** | The connection request has been sent to the partner. Waiting for them to accept or decline. |
| **Pending** | The partner has accepted, but your organisation still needs to approve the connection. |
| **Ready** | Both parties have accepted. The connection is active and you can collaborate on datasets and projects. |
| **Unavailable** | The connection cannot be established. The partner may not be listed on the platform. |
| **Deactivated** | One of the parties declined the connection request. To reconnect, delete the deactivated connection and submit a new request. |

---

## Managing connection actions

The Actions column provides two controls for each connection:

- **View** — Opens the connection detail view, showing the specifics of the relationship between your organisation and the connection partner.
- **Three-dot menu** — Depending on the connection status and your permissions, this allows you to **accept** or **request** a connection. These actions are only available to users with Editor (write access) permissions.

---

## Filtering and navigating connections

- Use **Filters** (top right of the table) to narrow connections by status or other attributes.
- Click **+ Add View** to create saved filter views for quick access.
- Use the **Items per page** dropdown at the bottom to adjust how many connections are shown per page.

## Subscription-based connections

When Omnisient sets up a subscription, a connection invitation must be accepted by both the **subscription owner** and the **subscription user** before it becomes active.

A subscription grants access to a specific project type — think of it as a pass for creating project templates (like ACE or Financial Inclusion), defining which project types are available and for how long.

### How subscription connections work

1. **Omnisient configures a subscription** linking the subscription owner and user. A connection invitation is sent to both parties.
2. **Both parties review** the connection details. The subscription user verifies the correct owner was connected, and the owner has visibility on who wants to use their subscription.
3. **Both parties accept independently.** If either party declines, the subscription will not be enabled and no data is shared.
4. **Once active**, the subscription user can create projects using the subscribed project type. An approval request is sent to the subscription owner before processing begins.

> For details on the approval process, see [Approving Projects](https://help.omnisient.com/en/articles/approving-projects).

---

## What to expect

After requesting a connection:

1. Your connection appears in the table with **Requested** status.
2. Once processed and sent, the status changes to **Awaiting**.
3. When the partner accepts, the status changes to **Pending** (awaiting your approval).
4. Once you approve, the status changes to **Ready** and you can begin creating projects and sharing datasets with the partner.
5. If either party declines, the connection moves to **Deactivated**. To try again, delete the deactivated connection and submit a new request.

## Common issues

| Issue | Likely cause | Fix |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| Connection stuck on Awaiting | Partner has not responded to the invitation | Contact your account manager to follow up with the partner |
| Connection shows Unavailable | Partner is not registered on Omnisient | Verify the partner is on the platform and submit a new request |
| Connection shows Deactivated | One of the parties declined the request | Delete the deactivated connection and submit a new request from scratch |
| Unable to approve a Pending connection | You do not have Editor/write permissions | Check your role with your organisation's admin |
| Connection request not appearing | Company name may have been entered incorrectly | Verify the spelling and submit a new request |
| No subscription invitation received | Subscription has not been configured by Omnisient | Contact your account manager to confirm the subscription setup |
