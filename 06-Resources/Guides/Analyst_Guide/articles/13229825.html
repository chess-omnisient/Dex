As part of our ongoing effort to help clients get the most out of their data collaborations, the Omnisient platform allows users to request SQLPad exports when they need to run further analysis or generate insights outside the platform. SQLPad exports provide a secure way to extract aggregated query results so you can continue your analysis in external tools, while ensuring all data shared through the export follows the same approval and security standards that apply across the platform.
​
The process is simple, secure, and designed to ensure that all exports go through the necessary partner and internal reviews before becoming available for download. Here’s how to create a SQLPad export.

Step 1: Navigate to the Project

Start by opening the project from which you want to create an export.
Click Open with, then select SQLPad.

Project types that currently support SQLPad exports include:

  • ACE

  • Overlap

  • Predictive Modelling on Retail Data

  • Financial Inclusion

Step 2: Build and Run Your Query

Use SQLPad to build and run your query as usual.
Once the query completes successfully, a download icon will appear (only for supported project types). Select the icon and then choose Request CSV when you are satisfied with the results you want to export.

Step 3: Submit Your SQLPad Export Request

After selecting Request CSV, a modal will open prompting you to provide a few details.

⚠️ Note: This request is sent to your collaboration partners for approval, followed by an additional security check by Omnisient. Once fully approved, your export will be available for download.

1. Sample of Records

A random sample of rows from your export will be shared with approvers to give them context and help them assess your request.

2. Aggregation Confirmation

You must confirm that Omnisient permits only the download of aggregated results.


By selecting this option, you acknowledge that your export does not contain information that could lead to the identification of any individual.

3. Terms & Conditions

Confirm that you’ve read and agree to Omnisient’s Terms and Conditions.

Once everything is complete and compliant, click Request Export to submit your request.

Step 4: Monitor Your Export’s Progress

You can track the status of all your SQLPad exports under the All Files section in the side navigation. Each export follows a clear lifecycle, and its current status will tell you exactly where it is in the process.

💡 Note: Only exports you have created will surface here.

Statues to expect and what they mean:

Processing / New

The export has been received and Omnisient is performing additional automated security checks.

Awaiting Approval

Your collaboration partners are reviewing your export.

💡 The Approvals column shows how many partners have approved so far.

Available

Your export has passed all reviews and is ready for download.

Rejected

A collaborator has rejected your export.


✉️ You’ll receive an email with the rejection reason so you can adjust and resubmit if needed.

Failed

The export did not pass Omnisient’s security checks.

✉️ An email will explain what failed and why.

Locked

The export has expired or the underlying project has locked (for example, due to a dataset share expiry).

🎉 And that’s it!

You’ve successfully created a SQLPad export.
This process ensures secure, transparent collaboration while enabling you to extract valuable insights from your projects.
