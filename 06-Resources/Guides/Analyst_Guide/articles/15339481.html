The dashboard is your launchpad on the Omnisient platform. It gives you an at-a-glance overview of your data universe, a summary of your recent work, and quick, customizable shortcuts to the tools you use most. This guide walks through each part of the dashboard so you can get the most out of it.

Getting around: the navigation menu

The Omnisient Web App is navigated using the menu on the left. Click any menu item to open its own sidebar, which contains the functionality available under that item.

For example, when Dashboard is selected in the left navigation, the dashboard view and its options are shown. The same pattern applies to other areas of the platform.

Interactive data widgets

The dashboard shows interactive widget tiles that surface key metrics and trends. Each tile has a dropdown, so you can choose which metric to display and view up to three at once. The available widgets are:

  • Industry Datasets: the number of datasets available to you, with an Available Datasets by Industry donut chart that breaks them down by sector (for example Bureau, Financial Services, Insurance, Leisure, Marketing Services, Retail, Technology, and Travel).

  • Unique Tokens: your total unique tokens available for matching, with a Unique Tokens by Identifying Fields bar chart split across IDs, phone numbers, and emails.

  • Dataset Reviews: the number of datasets awaiting review, with a Datasets Pending Review table listing each dataset name, who uploaded it, and when it was last updated. Select View to open a dataset.

  • Failed Uploads: the number of recent upload failures, with a Recent Upload Failures table showing the dataset name, who uploaded it, and when.

  • Shares by Company: the total number of shares, with a Total Shared Datasets by Company chart that compares datasets shared with us and shared by us for each company.

Choosing what each tile shows

Click the dropdown at the top of any widget tile and select the metric you want to see: Industry Datasets, Unique Tokens, Dataset Reviews, Failed Uploads, or Shares by Company. The tile updates to show that widget and its chart.

Your widget choices are remembered

When you select widgets and then navigate away, your choices are saved. When you return to the dashboard, the same widgets are displayed so you can pick up right where you left off.

Quick links: save your top 3 shortcuts

Quick links let you pin your three most-used destinations to the dashboard for one-click access. Your choices are personal to you and are saved between sessions.

How to set your quick links

  1. Open the Quick Links section on the dashboard and locate the three dropdown menus.

  2. Choose a link from the first dropdown, then repeat for the second and third dropdowns.

  3. Use your shortcuts. Your saved quick links now appear on the dashboard. Click any one to jump straight to that page or feature.

Recent projects

The dashboard shows your most recent projects as cards, so you can find and reopen active work quickly. Each card includes:

  • Project name: shown in full where possible. Long names are shortened with an ellipsis, and hovering over the name reveals the full title.

  • Status badge: a color-coded label showing the project state.

  • Date: the creation date, shown as DD MMM YYYY (for example, 12 Nov 2024).

  • Assigned users: the people who have access to the project.

  • Open Project link: opens the project directly. For projects that are Awaiting Approval or Failed, this link is disabled.

The right sidebar

The panel on the right keeps you informed about new data and recent activity.

Recently available datasets

See datasets that have recently been added to the platform by your organization, so you can quickly identify new resources available to you.

Activity

Review the latest activity across the platform for your organization, helping you stay up to date with recent changes and activity. Select View All Activity to see the full log.

Request a widget

Want to see something specific on your dashboard? Use the Request a Widget option to submit a request for a new dashboard widget. We use these requests to keep refining and expanding the dashboard, so please share what would help you.

Notifications

Notifications are surfaced prominently at the top of the dashboard so you can quickly spot important alerts and updates, and reduce the chance of missing anything essential.
