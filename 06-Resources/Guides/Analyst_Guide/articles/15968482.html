Approvals is where you control what partners can do with your shared data. When a partner creates a project or a SQLPad export using your dataset, the request goes to Approvals, where an Admin decides whether it's allowed to proceed.

🔐 Permissions

Only users with Admin permissions can access Approvals. See the User Management article.

Where to find Approvals

In the Omnisient Platform, go to Admin → Approvals. This is your central hub for reviewing every request.

You'll get an email notification when a new request comes in, with a direct link to review it.

Make sure to action requests as promptly. If a request isn't actioned in time, it will lock and the partner will be blocked.

Project approval

Approving projects created on your data gives you control, visibility, and peace of mind around how your data is being used. This process is designed to be simple and transparent, so you always stay in the loop.

What it means

How to review

  1. Find the request and click Review in the final column.

  2. Check the request details in the modal that opens. The review shows the request details, along with the shared dataset that the partner wants to use to create the project.

  3. Click Approve or Reject.

Action

Detail

Approve

The requestor gets a confirmation email and the request moves into processing.

Reject

A modal opens asking for a rejection reason. Be specific here. It appears in the requestor's rejection email, so a clear reason means they know exactly what to fix before resubmitting their request.

The review modal breaks the request into two sections:

Section

What it shows

Project Creation Details

The name of the partner, the project type, date of request and project code.

Shared Dataset

The shared dataset's name, dates, status, and who shared it.

SQLPad export request approval

Approving SQLPad exports is an important part of ensuring that your data is used responsibly and transparently. It gives you clear visibility into how your data contributes to insights, while keeping you in control of what gets exported.

Once a SQLPad export is approved, Omnisient can no longer track or control copies saved locally by the user.
