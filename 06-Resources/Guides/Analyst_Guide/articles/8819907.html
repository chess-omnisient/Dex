In this article, we will guide you through the process of retrying a project that has previously failed in Omnisient's platform. With our user-friendly interface, you now have the ability to easily rerun a project to ensure its success. Whether you're a user, admin, or developer, this guide will help you make the most of this feature.

Step-by-step Instructions:

  1. Begin by logging into your Omnisient account.

  2. Once you're logged in, navigate to the "Projects" section of the platform.

  3. Look for the project that you wish to retry. Projects that have failed will be clearly marked.

  4. To retry the project, locate the "Actions" menu or icon associated with the project.

  5. Click on the "Actions" icon to reveal a dropdown menu of options.

  6. From the dropdown menu, select "Retry Project."

  7. Confirm your action when prompted to proceed with retrying the project.

  8. The system will then initiate the process of rerunning the project. You will receive notifications and updates as the project progresses.

By following these simple steps, you can easily retry a project that has previously failed in Omnisient's platform. This feature streamlines the process of resolving issues and ensures that your projects run smoothly.
