An Overlap project lets you compare your customer dataset against a partner's shared dataset to identify where your customer bases intersect and where they don't. It's the most common starting point in a new data partnership, giving both parties a clear picture of shared and exclusive customers before committing to deeper collaboration.

Key concepts

Term

What it means

Overlap

Customers who appear in both your dataset and your partner's dataset.

Non-overlap

Customers who appear in only one dataset. Useful for identifying acquisition and expansion opportunities.

Customer Identifier

The PII field mapped to your dataset when it was registered. Used to match records between the two datasets during an overlap.

Denormalized dataset

A dataset with one row per customer. Overlap projects require this structure as data with multiple rows per customer must be flattened before use.

Segment

An additional attribute attached to customer records (e.g., spend tier, health score) used to filter and enrich overlap results.

Match types

When creating an Overlap project, you choose how records are matched between the two datasets.

Match type

How it works

When to use

Best match

The platform matches across multiple PII fields: phone number, first name, surname, and others to find the strongest possible match automatically.

Default. Use when you want the highest match rate.

ID match

Matching is performed on the ID field specifically.

Use when you want to match on a single, known identifier.

How Overlap works

  1. You select one of your own datasets and one shared partner dataset.

  2. The platform checks that both datasets share at least one common PII column. If none are found, you are warned before submitting.

  3. Records are matched using your chosen match type.

  4. The project is submitted for approval by the partner dataset owner.

  5. Once approved and processed, results are available in Power BI.

Results show:

  • The number and percentage of customers appearing in both datasets

  • Customers appearing only in your dataset

  • Customers appearing only in the partner's dataset — if the partner has chosen to share their non-overlap

Results can be filtered by any segments attached to either dataset.

What you can do with results

Capability

Detail

Understand your shared customer base

See how much of your customer base overlaps with a partner's — useful for evaluating partnership value before deeper collaboration.

Segmented analysis

Filter results by segments to answer questions like: "Of our shared customers, how many are high-value?" Segments must be uploaded as additional columns on the dataset.

Overlap-only sharing

Each party sees shared customers but not the other's exclusive customers. Protects acquisition strategy and marketing IP.

Full base sharing

Both parties see each other's complete customer breakdown. Supports comparative profiling and identifying untapped segments.

Selections

Create defined subsets of customers from results for use in activations or further analysis. Subject to dataset share agreement terms.

Limitations

Limitation

Detail

Transactional and multi-row data

Overlap is optimized for one record per customer. Data with multiple rows per customer (transactional, claims, diagnosis) must be aggregated to customer level before use. Contact your account manager if you need support with this.

PII matching

Both datasets must share at least one common PII column. If none are found, the overlap returns zero results. The platform warns you at setup before you submit.

Datasets fixed after creation

Once a project is created, the selected datasets cannot be changed. Create a new project to use different tables.

No custom reports

The Power BI report is based on a fixed template. Custom report configurations are not currently available.
