Partner datasets in the Omnisient platform refer to datasets that are shared between companies within a Connection. These datasets can be used for overlap analysis or data exchange without revealing Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Users can manage partner datasets in the Datasets Dashboard, where they can:

  • View datasets shared with their company by a Private Collaboration partner.

  • Share datasets with a Private Collaboration partner.

  • Conduct overlap analyses using multiple partner datasets or publicly available datasets​.

To request a partner shares a dataset with you, please reach out to our support team and supply details such as partner's name and reason you would like to use their dataset.
