The Partner Activity Log records every action taken with your partners, so you can trace what happened, when, and with whom in one place.

Where to find Partner Activity Log

In the Omnisient Platform, go to Data Collaboration → Partner Activity Log.

How Partner Activity Log works

Every entry includes five fields:

Field

Description

Partner

The partner company the entry relates to

Activity

The specific detail of what happened, such as dataset shares that have been declined, projects created, and project reports created

Source

The dataset involved in the activity

Action

The event that occurred

Date

When the action occurred

👉 Because the log entries span every connection in one view, you can compare activity across partners at a glance.
